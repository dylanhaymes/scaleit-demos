Questions homeowners ask us

How much does a roof replacement cost in Largo?
For a 1,500 sq ft Florida home, generally $8,000–$18,000. Shingle replacements land on the lower end; metal and tile cost more. Pitch, roof complexity, damaged decking, tear-off, and Pinellas County permit fees all factor in. Your free estimate breaks down your exact price before any work begins — no hidden fees.
How do I know if storm damage needs an inspection?
Look for missing, cracked, or curling shingles, granules collecting in gutters, water stains on ceilings or walls, sagging spots on the deck, or lifted flashing around vents and skylights. After a named storm — or if you spot any of those signs — schedule an inspection promptly. Water intrusion causes secondary damage to insulation, decking, and framing the longer it waits.
Which roofing material lasts longest in Florida?
Metal (40–70 years) and clay tile (50+ years) lead on lifespan. Metal also resists wind uplift, sheds rain well, and reflects heat to help cooling costs. Concrete tile runs 30–50 years, asphalt shingles 15–25, and TPO flat systems 15–30. Annual inspections and keeping gutters and the roof surface clear help any system reach its full life.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes — fully licensed and insured, including general liability and workers' compensation, as Florida requires for roofing contractors. Permits are pulled for every replacement, and we provide full documentation before a project starts. Every job is backed by warranties on both materials and workmanship.
