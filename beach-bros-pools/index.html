Frequently asked questions

Straight answers, straight from Beach Bros Pools.

How can I request a quote for pool cleaning?

Easy — use the estimate form on this page, call our service team at (727) 223-1124, or email info@beachbrospools.com. A team member will promptly get in touch to discuss your pool and provide a customized quote.

What areas do you service?

Throughout Tampa Bay: Largo, Tampa, Dunedin, Oldsmar, Trinity, Seminole, East Lake, Highpoint, Clearwater, Ridgecrest, Citrus Park, Palm Harbor, Feather Sound, Safety Harbor, Belleair, St. Petersburg, Tarpon Springs, Westchase, Town ’N’ Country, and Greater Carrollwood — across Pinellas, Hillsborough, and Pasco counties.

Are you insured and certified?

Yes. We carry general liability insurance, and our technicians are certified to service any pool in Florida, including commercial and public pools. Each technician holds either a Certified Pool Technician (CPT) or Certified Pool Operator (CPO) certification.

How long should I run my pool equipment?

For private residential pools, run your pump at least 6–8 hours a day. Because Tampa Bay gets hot, run it 8–12 hours a day in the warmest months.

Why is my pool water cloudy?

The most common causes are poor filtration, high pH, high alkalinity, and contaminants. Reach out for a quote and we’ll clear it up — and with weekly service, it’s unlikely to happen in the first place.

Why does pool chemistry balance matter?

Balanced chemistry keeps the water safe and healthy. Too little chlorine invites bacteria and unsanitary conditions; too many chemicals irritate eyes, skin, and hair. We test and balance on every visit so you don’t have to think about it.

My pool turned green. How fast can you fix it?

A green pool usually can’t be corrected in a single day, but with the right chemicals and equipment we can generally treat an algae bloom and have it back to blue within a week.
