Common questions

What roofing options do you offer?

Shingle, metal, tile, and flat roofing, plus repairs, replacements, installations, and inspections. The right option depends on your structure, existing roof condition, preferred materials, and budget.

Can you help with storm-damaged roofs?

Yes. We assess visible storm damage and discuss next steps — temporary protection, repairs, or further evaluation — and provide project documentation to support the insurance claim process. Coverage decisions are made by your insurer.

What warranties do you offer?

Workmanship and manufacturer warranty options depend on the project, roofing system, and materials selected. Ask our team for details before your project begins.

Do you offer financing?

Yes — financing is available through a third-party financial institution. Contact us to learn about options, eligibility, and the application process.

How does the process work?

It starts with a consultation and estimate. After approval, the project is scheduled, materials are coordinated, and the work is completed. Timeline depends on roof size, materials, weather, and inspection requirements.
