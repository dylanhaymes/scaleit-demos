Questions

Frequently asked

Straight answers — no sales pressure.

Do you really offer free estimates?

Yes — free, no-pressure estimates with a detailed written quote and no hidden fees. We inspect in person, show you photos, and explain your options.

What warranty do I get?

A lifetime workmanship warranty from Redefine Roofing, plus manufacturer warranty programs through our Atlas and CertainTeed certifications, including the Atlas Signature Select Roofing System.

Can you help with an insurance claim?

Yes. We document storm damage, meet the process head-on, and work directly with insurance companies to help get your home back to pre-storm condition.

Do you offer emergency service?

Yes — 24/7 emergency service with same-day emergency tarping. Call (813) 590-9050 any time.

Should I switch to a metal roof?

For many Florida homeowners, yes — metal is rated for 160–180 mph winds, can earn insurance discounts, and lasts 40–70 years. We’ll run the honest math for your home during your estimate.
