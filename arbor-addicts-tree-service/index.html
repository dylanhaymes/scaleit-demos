What we do

Full-service tree care

Residential and commercial properties across the greater Tampa Bay area. The crew that gives your estimate is the crew that shows up to do the work.

Tree Removal

Dead, diseased, storm-damaged, or just in the wrong place — we remove trees cleanly and safely, including tight backyard access and trees over homes, pools, and power lines.

  • Section-by-section rigging, never dropped whole near structures
  • Crane-assisted removal for tall palms when needed
  • Honest read first: if trimming will do, we say so

Trimming & Pruning

Expert trimming and pruning that keeps your trees healthy, safe, and looking right — including the palm care Florida properties need year-round.

  • Palm tree trimming & care
  • Canopy raising and storm-season prep cuts
  • Deadwood removal over roofs and driveways

Stump Grinding & Removal

We grind stumps below ground level so you can replant, sod, or just stop mowing around them. Quoted separately from removal, so you only pay for stump work you actually need.

  • Same-day grinding often available with removal
  • Bundle with a removal and save a trip

Storm Prep & Cleanup

Florida weather is the job. We prepare trees before storm season and clean up fast after — including 24/7 emergency response for trees on roofs and hazardous leaners.

  • Preventative storm preparation & maintenance
  • Emergency storm cleanup, day or night
  • Thorough cleanup — yard left cleaner than we found it
