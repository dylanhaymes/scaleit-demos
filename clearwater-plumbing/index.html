Our Services

From a leaky faucet to a full re-pipe, one team handles the whole job — no runaround, no subcontractor shuffle.

Sewer & Drain Cleaning

Clogged drains, sewer backups, and root problems cleared fast — with camera inspection and hydro jetting to find the real cause.

Water Heaters

Repair, replacement, and installation of tank and tankless water heaters for homes and businesses — sized right, installed to code.

Pipe Re-lining

Trenchless pipe re-lining that restores damaged lines from the inside — without tearing up your yard, driveway, or floors.

Water Treatment

Filtration, softening, and water quality systems that protect your pipes, your appliances, and your family.

Fixtures & Faucets

Sinks, toilets, showers, tubs, and faucets installed and repaired by professionals — clean work, no drips left behind.

Gas Piping

Safe, code-compliant gas line installation and repair for homes, restaurants, and commercial kitchens.

Grease Traps

Grease trap installation, cleaning, and service for restaurants and commercial kitchens — stay compliant, stay open.

Lift Stations & Pumps

Lift station service, sump pumps, and sewer pumps kept running — so your property stays dry when it matters most.

Repiping

Whole-home and commercial repiping with copper and modern piping systems — the permanent fix for chronic leaks and low pressure.

About us

Clearwater Plumbing Inc

Clearwater Plumbing Inc has provided quality plumbing service, repair, and installation in Clearwater, St. Petersburg, and throughout the Tampa Bay area since 1951. Our expertly trained technicians specialize in diagnosing and solving residential, commercial, and industrial plumbing problems.

Leaky pipe, clogged drain, burst line, or a water heater that quit — we show up, explain the problem in plain language, and fix it right the first time. That is why our motto has held for over 70 years: service is our salesman.
