Think your pool is leaking?

In west-central Florida, losing ⅛″ to ¼″ of water a day to evaporation is normal — wind, water features, and heating all change the number. Before you call, run this simple 24-hour test from our leak team:

1

Fill it up

Fill the pool to its normal level — halfway up the skimmer mouth.

2

Mark the level

Put a piece of duct tape next to the skimmer at the water line.

3

Wait 24 hours

Run the pump as you normally would. Measure the loss as close to 24 hours later as you can, and note any rainfall.

4

Compare

Losing more than about ¼″ a day? That's more than evaporation — it's time to find the leak.
