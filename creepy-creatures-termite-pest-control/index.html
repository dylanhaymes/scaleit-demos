5 costly mistakes homeowners make hiring pest control

Before you call anyone — including us — here's what the big national chains don't want you to find out.

1

Choosing the cheapest quote

The cheapest company often uses the cheapest chemicals — which means they'll be back, and billing you, in 30 days. A proper treatment done right the first time costs less in the long run.

2

A different tech every visit

Your home isn't a number. The person treating it should know its history, trouble spots, and quirks. We assign the same expert to your home, every visit.

Signing before an honest inspection

Some companies “find” problems that don't exist to sell treatments you don't need. One of our five-star reviews is from a homeowner who thought they had termites — we told them they didn't.

No written guarantee

If a company won't stand behind their work in writing, what does that tell you? We guarantee our work at 110% — and we'll pay you $25 if you're not happy.

Not asking who shows up
