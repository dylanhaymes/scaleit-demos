From Our Blog

Chlorine vs. salt — what’s the difference?

A quick version of our guide. Either way, regular testing and balancing is what keeps the water safe and clear — and that’s included in every Cover2Pools visit.

Chlorine pools

The most common type. Chlorine is added directly — as tablets, granules or liquid — to kill bacteria, viruses and other microorganisms.

  • Lower upfront cost, familiar maintenance
  • Requires regular balancing to stay sanitized
  • Chlorine levels checked on every visit

Saltwater pools

Instead of adding chlorine directly, a salt chlorine generator produces chlorine from salt in the water.

  • Higher upfront investment for the generator
  • Salt cell needs periodic cleaning or replacement
  • Still needs pH, alkalinity and mineral checks

Frequently asked questions

What does a regular visit include?

Every visit works through our checklist: brushing and scrubbing, vacuuming, netting and skimming, skimmer and basket cleaning, filter cleaning, and testing pH, chlorine, alkalinity and mineral content — then balancing the water so it stays clear.

Do you service commercial and condo pools?

Yes. Cover2Pools provides residential and commercial pool services, including condo pools — one Google reviewer notes we’ve serviced their Tierra Verde condo pool for six months.

Which areas do you cover?

Pinellas County and Hillsborough County, including Seminole, Clearwater, Largo, St. Petersburg, Dunedin, Pinellas Park, Tarpon Springs and the beach communities listed above.

Why does water balance matter so much?

Balanced pH and alkalinity prevent corrosion to metal fixtures and surfaces, correct chlorine levels sanitize the water and control algae, and mineral checks reduce calcium build-up that deteriorates pool surfaces and equipment.
