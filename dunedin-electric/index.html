Our story

Built on honesty, integrity & discipline

Brad Martin founded Dunedin Electric in 1987 after leading electrical projects in the U.S. Navy. That discipline became the company's standard. Today it's a second-generation family business: Brad's son Wes — a Florida State Certified Electrical Contractor since 2008 — runs day-to-day operations, with his wife Jill in management and brother William having run office administration.

  • ✓

    Word-of-mouth reputation. We grew from a two-man specialty shop to 20+ electricians and a full fleet — mostly on referrals.

    No upsell. You get the straight facts and only the work you actually need.

    Customers first. The principle the company was founded on, on every job since 1987.
