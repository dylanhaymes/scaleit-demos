Clearbloom — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 17, 2026 · Developer: Scale It Designs · Contact: nijalgangsta@gmail.com

Clearbloom is a block puzzle game. This policy describes what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

What we collect

  • If you play as a guest: your display name, a 6-character friend code, your game progress (level/XP, games played, best score), saved games, settings, and cosmetic choices. This stays on your device unless you sign in.
  • If you sign in with Google: your display name, friend code, progress, and leaderboard scores are saved to your account on our server (Google Firebase, stored in the United States) so they follow you across devices and can appear on leaderboards. Your Google account email is held by Firebase Authentication to identify your account; it is shown in the app so you can see which account is signed in, and it is never written to our game database, never shown to other players, and never shared.
  • Friends: your display name and friend code are listed in a public friend-code directory so another player can find you by code. Friend requests hold both players' names, the code, and a 7-day expiry. Challenges hold the shared game seed and each player's score.
  • Leaderboards: your display name, score, lines cleared, best combo, and the date or week of the score. You can hide yourself from the global leaderboards in Settings.
  • Analytics: none leaves your device. The game keeps a small local log of gameplay events (at most 500 entries) in your device's storage. There is no analytics backend connected.

Ads

The web version of Clearbloom shows no real ads. In the Android app, ads are served by Google AdMob. Ads appear only at the end of some games — never during play, never after your first game of a session, and never more than one in three games. The game never sends your name, email, scores, or friend list to advertisers.

AdMob itself may collect your device's advertising ID and related device and ad-interaction data, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Before ads load, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings at any time.

In-app purchases

There are none yet. The in-game store is a test display only: nothing can be bought, no payment details are collected, and no charge can occur. If real purchases are added later, this policy will be updated first.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not ask for your age, date of birth, address, or phone number.
  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, or files from your device.
  • We do not share your data with any third party other than Google Firebase (account, game data, hosting) and Google AdMob (ads in the Android app), as described above.

Deleting your data

In the game, go to Settings → Delete account. That deletes your profile, your friend-code entry, your leaderboard scores, and your challenges from the server, deletes your sign-in account, and clears your saved data from the device. (Pending friend requests are not deleted by this step, but they expire automatically after 7 days.) Deleting needs a recent sign-in; if your session is old, sign out and back in first, then delete. You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing nijalgangsta@gmail.com from the email address on the account.

Children

Clearbloom is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what is described above, and we do not ask for a child's age or date of birth. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has shared information with us, contact us at the email above and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here and the effective date updated. Continued use of the game after a change means you accept the new policy.
